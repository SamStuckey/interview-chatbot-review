# Referral program: frequently asked questions

<!-- Curated by hand from "Carton Caps Referral FAQs.pdf". Where the FAQ

assumption to confirm with Carton Caps. Those places are marked "Per the
Program Rules". -->

**What is the referral program?**
You invite friends to join the Carton Caps app. When a friend signs up with
your referral code or link and completes the qualifying steps, you both get a
bonus in your Carton Caps accounts.

**How do I refer a friend?**
1. Tap the account icon to see account options.
2. Tap **Invite Friends**.
3. Copy your referral code and send it to your friend, or share a link with
   the buttons in the **Share Now** section.
4. Your friend installs the app with your link, or signs up with your code.

**What does my friend experience?**
Referred friends get an onboarding experience that introduces Carton Caps and
how it supports schools. They're also told about the bonus you'll both get.

**When do we get the bonus?**
Per the Program Rules: after your friend completes onboarding and performs a
qualifying action, such as making their first eligible product scan or linking
their account to a school.

**What kind of bonus is it?**
It varies over time: extra Carton Caps points or a special in-app reward. The
current bonus is shown on the referral page in the app.

**Can I refer more than one person?**
Yes. There's no limit, and you earn a bonus for each successful referral.

**My friend forgot to use my code or link. Can we still get the bonus?**
Per the Program Rules: your friend can use your code or link during sign-up,
or within a set window after first installing the app (the Rules give 48
hours only as an example; the actual window isn't stated). After that, the
referral isn't credited automatically.

**Can I refer someone who already uses Carton Caps?**
No. Referrals are only for people new to Carton Caps. Existing users, and
people who uninstall and reinstall the app, aren't eligible.

**How do I track my referrals?**
In the **Refer a Friend** section of the app you can see each referral's
status: signed up, completed onboarding, and whether your bonus was awarded.

**Why haven't I received my bonus yet?**
Your friend may not have finished onboarding or the qualifying action yet. If
it's been more than 48 hours and you think something's wrong, contact Carton
Caps support in the app.

**Are there restrictions?**
Yes. Carton Caps can withhold bonuses or disable accounts for fraud or abuse,
including self-referrals, spamming and fake accounts.
