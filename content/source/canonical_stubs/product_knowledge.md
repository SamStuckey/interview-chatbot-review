# Carton Caps app: product knowledge

## How Carton Caps works
People link their account to a school they want to support, then buy
participating products. Purchases earn Carton Caps, which raise money for the
linked school.

## Linking a school
Users choose a school to support in their account settings. Some referral
bonuses require the referred friend to link a school.

## Scanning receipts
Users scan their receipts in the app to record purchases of participating
products. A referred friend's first eligible product scan can count as their
qualifying action for the referral bonus.

## Bonus Caps
Bonus Caps are limited-time promotions that earn extra Caps on specific
products. Current Bonus Caps are listed in the app; Capper can show the active
ones as cards.

## The referral page
The **Invite Friends** / **Refer a Friend** section shows the user's referral
code, sharing buttons, the current referral bonus, and each referral's status.

## Getting help from support
For anything Capper can't help with (account problems, missing bonuses,
technical issues), users can contact Carton Caps support from the app: open
the account menu and choose **Help & Support**. This path is an assumption for
the demo, to confirm with Carton Caps.
