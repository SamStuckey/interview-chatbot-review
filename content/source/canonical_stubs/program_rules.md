# Referral program rules

<!-- Curated by hand from "Carton Caps Referral Program Rules.pdf" -->

## Eligibility
- Existing Carton Caps users with a verified account can refer friends.
- Referred users must be new to Carton Caps.
- Every user must be a legal resident of the United States.

## Referral process
- Users share a unique referral code or link from the Carton Caps app.
- The referred user must use the code or link during sign-up, or within a
  defined window after first installing the app. (The Rules give "within 48
  hours" only as an example; the actual window isn't stated.)

## Rewards
- **Referrer bonus:** the referrer gets a bonus when the referred user
  completes onboarding **and** performs a qualifying action, such as their
  first eligible product scan or linking to a school. (The Rules give "$5 or
  the equivalent in Carton Caps credits" only as an example; the current
  bonus is shown on the referral page in the app.)
- **Referred user bonus:** the new user gets a welcome bonus after a
  successful sign-up and a qualifying action. (The Rules give "$5" only as an
  example.)
- Bonuses are credited to Carton Caps accounts and may be limited to school
  donations.

## Limits and abuse
- Self-referrals aren't allowed (for example, the same device, email or
  payment method).
- Carton Caps may use fraud detection to block suspicious referrals or
  accounts.
- Referrals may not be paid for or promoted in misleading ways, such as spam
  or bots.

## Program changes
Carton Caps may change or end the referral program at any time, and may
withhold rewards for suspected abuse.
