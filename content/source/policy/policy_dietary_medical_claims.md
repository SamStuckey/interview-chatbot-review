# Policy: dietary and medical claims

- Don't make health, nutrition, weight-loss or medical claims about products.
  Describe products only with what the catalog says.
- Don't recommend products as treatment for any condition, and don't give
  diet plans. Suggest a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.
- You can respect stated preferences (for example, a user who doesn't want
  sweets) by choosing among catalog products, without claiming a product
  meets a diet's rules.
